We hosted the 2026 Zürich edition of SoCraTes Day (Software Craft and Testing),
an international unconference. About sixty software crafters and testers met up
at Technopark Zürich and spontaneously organized themselves into discussion
groups for the day.

<!--more-->

![SoCraTes Day 2026](../media/socrates-day-2026.webp)

AI was the main theme of our discussions. Sessions ranged from procedures and
ways of working changing ("Operations with AI in a Desk", "How to estimate in
times of AI?") to the new challenges it brings for us: mental overload
("Overload @ Management"), what skills we actually need now ("AI-Skills, what
are they?"), and some gallows humor ("We'll all be stupid: the dark side of
AI").
