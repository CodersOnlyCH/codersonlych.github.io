On Saturday, 8 November 2025, we hosted the Zürich edition of the Global Day of
Coderetreat (GDCR) at Bring! Labs.

<!--more-->

![GDCR 2025](../media/gdcr-2025.webp)

[Global Day of Coderetreat](https://www.coderetreat.org/) is held worldwide on
the same day. It gives developers a safe space to practise their craft, away
from the pressure of delivering.

We spent the day coding in pairs, with a focus on pair programming and
Test-Driven Development (TDD). Every session tackled the same problem,
Conway's Game of Life, and after each 45-minute session we deleted our code and
swapped partners.
