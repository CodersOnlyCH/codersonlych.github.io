On Saturday, 7 November 2026, we hosted the Zürich edition of the Global Day of
Coderetreat (GDCR) at Wingtra.

<!--more-->

![GDCR 2026](../media/gdcr-2026.webp)

[Global Day of Coderetreat](https://www.coderetreat.org/) is held worldwide on
the same day. It gives developers a safe space to practise their craft, away
from the pressure of delivering.

We spent the whole day coding in pairs, with a focus on pair programming and
Test-Driven Development (TDD). We ran six sessions on the same problem,
Conway's Game of Life, each with different constraints. After every session we
deleted our code and started fresh, and each one ended with a feedback round on
what we had learned.

We were a small group this year, but the dynamics and the learning were as good
as with a larger crowd.
